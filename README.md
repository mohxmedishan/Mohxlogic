# mohxlogic

**Understanding computing from the logic up.**

mohxlogic is my personal space for exploring, understanding, experimenting with, and teaching computer science — from the smallest logic gates to complex systems.

This is where theory meets experimentation. I break concepts down, build circuits, test ideas, document discoveries, and explain how things actually work instead of just memorizing what they do.

## What you'll find

- **Digital Logic** — Logic gates, truth tables, Boolean algebra, logic expressions, and simplification.
- **Circuit Design** — Combinational and sequential circuits, adders, subtractors, multiplexers, decoders, and more.
- **Computer Architecture** — How processors, registers, memory, buses, and instruction systems work.
- **Binary & Number Systems** — Binary, hexadecimal, conversions, signed numbers, and two's complement.
- **Computer Science** — Concepts, algorithms, computational thinking, and the foundations of computing.
- **Experiments** — Testing ideas, building circuits, breaking things down, and figuring out why they work.
- **Projects** — Practical implementations of concepts, from simple logic circuits to more complex systems.

## The approach

I believe the best way to understand a system is to build it yourself.

Rather than treating computing as a black box, mohxlogic explores what's happening underneath — how individual components connect, how logic creates functionality, and how simple operations combine into increasingly complex systems.

Every concept should be understandable. Every circuit should have a reason. Every experiment should teach something.

## The goal

To create a growing collection of explanations, experiments, interactive projects, and circuits that make computer science easier to understand — while documenting my own journey of learning, building, and exploring.
